Get the Smart Irrigation app

Watch and control the Pinamungahan Automated Farm from your phone or computer. Same account, same readings, same controls as the website.

You're already using the app. This page is for setting it up on another phone or computer.

This device

Android phone or tablet

Download the app Version 1.0 · 1.7 MB · Android 7 or newer

The Android download isn't available right now. In Chrome, open the menu (⋮) and choose Add to Home screen › Install: you get the same app.

  1. Tap Download the app. If Chrome asks, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If the phone asks, allow installing apps from Chrome (Settings › Allow from this source), then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect says it doesn't know this app, tap More details › Install anyway: it isn't from the Play Store, that's all.
  4. Open Smart Irrigation and sign in with your usual account.
This device

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open citu-smart-irrigation.web.app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share , then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Smart Irrigation is now on your Home Screen; sign in once inside it.

Apple only lets App Store apps be downloaded, so on an iPhone the app is added from Safari instead. It works the same.

This device

Windows, Mac or Chromebook

  1. Open citu-smart-irrigation.web.app in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the Install icon at the right end of the address bar (or the browser's menu › Install Smart Irrigation).
  3. It opens in its own window, with an icon on the Start menu, desktop or dock.

Or just use the website

Nothing to install: open https://citu-smart-irrigation.web.app in any browser.
